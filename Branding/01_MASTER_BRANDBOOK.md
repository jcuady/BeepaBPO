# Beepa BPO — Master Brandbook

**Version:** 1.0  
**Domain:** `beepabpo.com`  
**Brand Direction:** Modern Human Corporate  
**Core Concept:** Connected Growth

---

# 1. Brand Purpose

Beepa exists to create meaningful progress through people and partnerships.

The company began from a deeply personal purpose: building something meaningful for a family's future. That purpose now extends to the Filipino professionals who find opportunities through Beepa and the clients who depend on Beepa to build capable, dependable teams.

Beepa must never be presented as simply a source of cheaper labor. The brand should communicate **trust, opportunity, capability, partnership, visibility, and sustainable growth**.

---

# 2. Brand Positioning

## Positioning Statement

> **Beepa is a people-first outsourcing partner helping businesses build dependable teams and grow with confidence.**

## Why This Positioning

It gives Beepa room to serve different industries and functions without becoming trapped as only a virtual-assistant provider, call center, or one-service outsourcing company.

Beepa should be positioned as an **outsourcing partner**, not only a staffing vendor.

---

# 3. Brand Promise

Beepa should consistently deliver five promises:

1. **Dependable people** — capable professionals who can become an extension of the client's organization.
2. **Reliable partnership** — Beepa remains involved after placement and supports the relationship.
3. **Operational visibility** — clients gain confidence through clear attendance, timesheets, reporting, requests, and communication.
4. **Flexible growth** — teams and workflows can evolve as client needs change.
5. **Human support** — employees and clients are treated as people, not account numbers.

---

# 4. Brand Pillars

## People

Communicate opportunity, respect, belonging, professional growth, and collaboration.

## Partnership

Communicate transparency, reliability, communication, accountability, and long-term working relationships.

## Progress

Communicate capacity, improvement, employee growth, client growth, and sustainable business growth.

## Purpose

Beepa has a real founding story. Use it authentically and with restraint. Do not exploit the story as sentimental advertising.

---

# 5. Core Brand Messages

## Primary Corporate Line

# People. Partnership. Progress.

Use for corporate branding, presentations, social profiles, footer branding, and high-level company communication.

## Brand Story Line

# Built on Purpose. Growing Together.

Use for the About page, careers, culture, anniversary content, employer branding, and founder-story communication.

## Marketing Message

# Better Teams. Stronger Businesses.

Use selectively on service pages, campaign pages, sales materials, and conversion-oriented messaging.

Do not place all three statements on the same section or design.

# 8. Visual Direction

## Modern Human Corporate

Beepa combines:

- The structure and credibility of professional services.
- The warmth and authenticity of a people-centered organization.
- The simplicity and efficiency of a modern digital product.
- A proprietary visual language derived from the Beepa logo.

## Connected Growth

The interlocking Beepa symbol is the visual foundation.

It can represent:

- Beepa + Client
- People + Business
- Talent + Opportunity
- Partnership + Progress
- Legacy + Future

Do not introduce unrelated decorative systems when logo geometry can solve the same visual problem.

# 9. Core Color System

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Beepa Navy | `#1F2058` | Trust, authority, corporate foundation |
| Beepa Green | `#119446` | Growth, people, progress, primary accent |
| Beepa Lime | `#93C63D` | Energy, momentum, micro-emphasis |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clarity, openness, primary surface |
| Deep Ink | `#17182B` | High-readability body text |
| Slate | `#667085` | Secondary text |
| Soft Gray | `#EAECF0` | Borders and dividers |
| Mist | `#F5F7F6` | Alternate surface |
| Soft Green | `#EEF7E8` | Brand-tinted subtle surface |

### Recommended Public-Site Balance

- 60% white / neutral
- 25% navy
- 10% green
- 5% lime

Lime must never become a dominant background or the default text color.

# 10. Typography

## Manrope

Use for:

- Hero headlines
- H1–H4
- Major statistics
- Buttons
- Short marketing statements
- Important dashboard metrics

Preferred weights: 500 / 600 / 700.

## Inter

Use for:

- Body copy
- Forms
- Tables
- Dashboards
- CRM
- Attendance
- Payroll
- HR
- Client portal
- Employee PWA
- Captions and metadata

Preferred weights: 400 / 500 / 600.

### Principle

Manrope expresses the brand. Inter carries the work.

# 11. Public Website Personality

The public site should feel:

- Professional
- Human
- Editorial
- Bright
- Confident
- Spacious
- Contemporary

Approximate character mix:

- 40% professional services
- 30% human/editorial storytelling
- 20% modern digital product quality
- 10% distinct Beepa graphic identity

Do not reverse this and build a SaaS landing page with people added afterward.

---

# 12. Product / PWA Personality

The application should feel:

- Calm
- Fast
- Structured
- Reliable
- Task-focused
- Data-aware
- Accessible

The product should use the same design tokens as the website but with reduced decoration and higher information density.

Marketing website = emotion + credibility + storytelling.  
Application = clarity + action + visibility.

# 15. Graphic Language

Use the Beepa mark as visual DNA.

Primary devices:

1. **Interlocking Frames** — image frames, content framing, relationship diagrams.
2. **Cropped Curves** — large-scale section accents and composition anchors.
3. **Connected Lines** — subtle process or relationship graphics.
4. **Brand Pattern** — simplified mark geometry at approximately 3–8% opacity.

Never use all devices together in one section.

---

# 16. UI Style

Prefer:

- White and neutral surfaces
- Navy hierarchy
- Green actions and active states
- Lime micro-highlights
- 8px-based spacing
- Subtle borders
- Low-elevation shadows
- Medium corner radii
- Strong content hierarchy
- Consistent icon family

Avoid:

- Bubble UI
- Huge pills everywhere
- Neon gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Heavy shadows
- Decorative charts
- Generic shadcn default styling

---

# 17. Motion

Motion communicates **connection and progress**.

Use GSAP/ScrollTrigger only for high-value storytelling moments:

- Founder timeline
- Connected-growth brand sequence
- Selected service stories
- Company-growth milestones

Use lighter CSS or component transitions for product microinteractions.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

# 18. Experience Principles

## Client Website

The user should think:

1. This looks professional.
2. They understand people and outsourcing.
3. They feel credible and transparent.
4. Their approach is differentiated.
5. I want to speak with them.

## Employee PWA

The user should immediately know:

1. What is my schedule?
2. Am I clocked in?
3. What needs my attention?
4. How much leave do I have?
5. Where is my payroll/payslip?

## Client Portal

The user should immediately know:

1. What is happening with my team?
2. What requires my approval?
3. Where are my reports and requests?
4. How do I contact Beepa?

## Admin

The user should immediately know:

1. What requires action today?
2. What is abnormal?
3. What is waiting for approval?
4. What trend matters?

# 19. Non-Negotiable Design Rules

1. Do not make Beepa look like a generic SaaS company.
2. Do not make Beepa look like a traditional call center.
3. Do not use all brand colors equally.
4. Do not overuse lime.
5. Do not use decorative gradients unless they are extremely restrained and brand-derived.
6. Do not use arbitrary colors as decoration.
7. Do not place every piece of information inside a card.
8. Do not use icons when typography or hierarchy is clearer.
9. Do not use motion merely to make the site look expensive.
10. Do not copy a component library's default visual identity.
11. Do not treat mobile as a shrunken desktop.
12. Do not expose client or employee data across tenants.
13. Do not let role-specific administration replace employee self-service; internal admins are employees too.
14. Accessibility is part of the design system, not a final QA phase.
15. Content clarity takes priority over decoration.

---

# 20. Final Approval Question

Before approving any Beepa output, ask:

- Does this feel credible to an international client?
- Does this still feel human?
- Is the information immediately understandable?
- Does it look unmistakably Beepa?
- Is it scalable across website, PWA, admin, careers, and marketing?
- Is the design helping the content rather than distracting from it?

If any answer is no, revise it.
