## ⚠️ Publishing note for the two hiring posts

The people in Posts 6 and 7 are AI-generated, not real Beepa employees. The brandbook (§ 14, § 11Content QA) requires **real** employee photography and explicitly forbids fabricating people, testimonials, or staff portrayals. A synthetic "employee" on a hiring post is a live trust risk: an applicant who spots it will assume the whole recruiting process is fake, and it contradicts the brandbook's rule against synthetic office scenes.

**Publish-safe path:** shoot one real Beepa employee in the navy uniform (10 minutes, phone camera, near a window), then swap the photo into either layout. Keep the layout, headline, CTA and gradient — only the photo changes.

**If you must ship as-is:** don't caption the person by name or role, and never reuse the same face in a testimonial context.

## General posting rules for BeepaBPO

- **Never** post all three brand lines (People. Partnership. Progress. / Built on Purpose. Growing Together. / Better Teams. Stronger Businesses.) in the same post. One per post.
- **Never** use "world-class," "best-in-class," "cutting-edge," "synergy," "disrupt," or "revolutionary." The brandbook bans these.
- **Never** use "Resources" or "Seats" to describe people. Use "Team," "Team members," "Professionals," "Specialists," "Talent."
- **Never** use the word "Manpower" in marketing copy. Brandbook says to avoid.
- **Always** include a specific CTA. Not "Learn More." Use "Talk to our team," "Build your team," "See how we work," or "See open roles."
- **Always** add `beepabpo.com` once per post.
- **Always** credit real photos to the team in the photo (not "stock") when they become identifiable in our CMS.
- **Always** include alt text on the image: "Beepa team collaborating in a bright Manila office" or similar — never "stock photo."
- **UTM tracking:** append `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=[post-slug]` to the link.
